## Frequently Asked Questions

### My reminder didn't fire.
Check that notifications are allowed for Orbit in **Settings → Notifications → Orbit**. Orbit uses *time-sensitive* local notifications, which require Notifications to be enabled and also the **Time Sensitive Notifications** toggle to be on (same screen). Reminders with a trigger time in the past are saved but not scheduled — you'll see an orange "Not scheduled" badge under the reminder.

### The event isn't showing up in the iOS Calendar.
Adding to Calendar is optional and user-triggered. Open the event, tap the **Add to Calendar** row at the top, and grant Calendar access if prompted. You can later sync changes or remove the Calendar entry from the same row or the ⋯ menu.

### I tapped a reminder notification — where did it take me?
Tapping a reminder opens Orbit directly on the event the reminder belongs to. If the event was deleted, the app opens Home instead.

### How do I add an attachment?
Inside an event, scroll to the **Attachments** section (or the section of a specific module like Transport/Stay/Ticket), tap **Add Attachment**, and choose **Choose File…** or **Choose Photo…**. Attachments are stored locally inside the app.

### How do I delete an event?
Swipe left on any event in the Home list, or open the event and use ⋯ → **Delete Event**. To delete multiple events, tap the ⋯ menu on Home → **Select Events**, pick the ones you want, then tap the red trash icon in the bottom bar.

### Does Orbit sync across my devices?
No. Orbit is intentionally **local-first**. Your data stays on the device you entered it on. There is no iCloud sync, no account, and no backend.

### How do I back up my events?
Orbit's data is included in a standard **iCloud Backup** or **Finder/iTunes encrypted backup** of your device. Restoring the backup restores your Orbit events.

### How do I request a refund?
Orbit is currently free. If that changes in the future, App Store refunds are handled by Apple — visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) and select the purchase.

### The app crashes or freezes.
Force-quit Orbit and relaunch. If the issue persists, make sure iOS and the app are up to date, then email us with the details listed above.
