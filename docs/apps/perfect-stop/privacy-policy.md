*Last updated: April 20, 2026*

Tiago Melo ("we", "our", or "us") built The Perfect Stop ("the App") as a commercial application. This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of information when you use the App.

## Information We Do Not Collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit any personal information. The App does not require you to create an account or provide any personal data.

## Advertising

The App displays ads provided by Google AdMob. Google may collect and use data to serve ads, including:

- Device identifiers
- IP address
- General location (not precise)
- App usage data related to ad interaction

We request non-personalized ads by default. For more information on how Google uses data, visit [Google's Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

The App uses Apple's SKAdNetwork for ad attribution measurement, which does not track individual users.

## Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children. Since no personal data is collected from any user, the App is safe for use by all ages.

## Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
