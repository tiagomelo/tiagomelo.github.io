## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I restore my "Remove Ads" purchase on a new device?
Open the app, go to the shop/settings screen, and tap **Restore Purchases**. Make sure you are signed in to the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.

### The ad tracking permission prompt did not appear / I want to change my choice.
Open **Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking** on your device. From there you can allow or deny tracking for Perfect Stop at any time.

### A rewarded ad did not grant my reward.
Rewards are granted only when the ad finishes playing. If the ad failed to load or was dismissed early, try again after a few seconds. If the problem persists, email us with your device and app version.

### The game crashes or freezes.
Force-quit the app and relaunch. If it persists, make sure iOS and the app are up to date, then email us with the details listed above.

### How do I request a refund?
Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. Visit [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com) and select the purchase.
